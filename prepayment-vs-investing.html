HOW THIS COMPARISON WORKS

Equal cash flow. One original mortgage horizon.

The comparison avoids giving the prepayment path a hidden advantage simply because the loan finishes earlier.

Fair-comparison sequence

  1. Build the original mortgage schedule from the entered balance, rate, term and repayment cadence.
  2. Apply the chosen lump sum or recurring extra to principal in the Prepay path.
  3. After early payoff, redirect scheduled payments that would otherwise have continued — plus any continuing recurring extra money — into the Prepay-path investment account.
  4. In the Invest path, keep the original mortgage schedule and invest the same planned extra cash instead.
  5. Compare both paths at the original scheduled mortgage payoff date and solve the effective annual return where they are approximately equal.

Important limitations

  • No investment product or market return is recommended.
  • No automatic tax, lender-penalty or legal-rule calculation.
  • No home-price forecast is needed because the home is the same asset in both paths.
  • Variable rates, offset/redraw features and product-specific restrictions can change real outcomes.

FAQ

Questions about prepaying versus investing

Why is the break-even return the main result?

It gives you a transparent threshold under the assumptions you entered without Carrowmont pretending to know future market returns.

Why does the Prepay option start investing after the mortgage is cleared?

Because the regular payment would otherwise have continued until the original payoff date. Redirecting that freed-up cash keeps the same planned cash flow for both options until that date.

Does Carrowmont include tax or investment fees?

Not automatically. If you want those effects reflected, use an effective annual investment return that already reflects the fees or tax assumptions you choose to model.

Should I use all spare cash for either prepayment or investing?

Not necessarily. Liquidity, emergency reserves and high-cost debt can matter before deciding how much cash is truly available. This tool is educational and does not perform a suitability assessment.
